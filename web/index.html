About TPT Inventory

TPT Inventory is an offline barcode stock counting app by TPT Solutions.

Privacy: your SKU list and scan counts are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB), on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Once loaded, the app keeps working with no internet connection — export a CSV at the end of your count to back up or reconcile.

Supports QR, Data Matrix, Code 128, Code 39, EAN-13, and UPC-A.

Questions or feedback: visit tptsolutions.co.nz.
